# Wing 4: AI Pet & Family Portrait App (Shopify + Printify Direct Shipping)
- **Wing**: `wing_pet_portraits` | **Room**: `room_pet_portrait_app` | **Drawer**: `drawer_product_and_customer_vault`
- **Category**: `projects` | **Season**: `FALL` | **Updated**: 2026-10-22T12:25:00-07:00

1. New customers get a free upfront set of credits upon email + password signup.
2. Upload pet/people photos -> select 3 different styles -> AI renders multiple variations per style.
3. Combine multiple pets or people into one portrait, change backgrounds, remove objects.
4. Pick favorites + select exact product (canvas, framed print, mug, blanket, apparel) with real-time live preview on the product; fulfilled via Shopify/Printify direct shipping.
5. Persistent email/password account library saves all uploaded & generated photos for future orders without paying for more credits to re-render.
